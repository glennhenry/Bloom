**Bloom** is a social portal platform that is similar to website like Yahoo, Naver, MSN. The platform deliver information through various internet services and provide social content. It strives to be a place that is lively, cozy, and warm.

Bloom is a "portal" to (illustrative):

- Internet
  - Bloom find (search)
  - Bloom news
  - Bloom tools (various utilities and tools)
- Services
  - Bloom forecast (weather report)
  - Bloom finance (stock)
  - Bloom maps
  - Bloom market (e-shopping)
- Messaging
  - Bloom mail (email service)
- Community
  - Bloom network (reddit-like forum)
  - Bloom blog
  - Bloom talk (live chat)
- Entertainment
  - Bloom ask (quiz & polls)
  - Bloom games

And most importantly:

- Bloom K-pop: a special K-pop fan community section.
  - Cafe: an alternative traditional vbulletin-like forum

### Objectives

The platform tries to replicate the experience of old social portal applications. The reason behind this project is also for engineering exploration.

The platform strives to be:

- A place to obtain information.
- A place relied for internet necessity.
- A place people visit repeatedly to hangout and feel social presence.

The platform rely on noisy contents that aren't necessarily important to users, but add atmospheric value to the website.

### Overview

5 important points about the platform.

#### Portal

A portal application implements a gateway to various internet services. This platform inherits that principle to provide anything someone would need on the internet.

Including the main services listed above, other portal utility features:

- World clock / time service
- Various internet utilities
  - time calculator and converter
  - unit converter
  - stopwatch, countdown timer
  - personal notepad
  - random tools (generator, picker, wheel)
- Various arcade games which can be singleplayer or multiplayer
  - Billiard, poker, monopoly
  - Card games, word games, daily puzzle

Bloom K-pop can inherit some of the generic portal element and transform it into something relevant for K-pop fans. For example, a calendar listing the month's schedule, comeback date, anniversary; a widget to provide chart updates or live events countdown.

#### Profile & Community

User joins the platform and optionally create an account. The single account is used for the entire platform services. A user profile can be customized for personalization.

Profile:

- Each user has a page they can decorate.
- Profile includes personal information like username, display name, nationality, bio, hobby, etc.
- A guestbook where other users may leave a post on someone's profile.

Various community:

- Cafe: A central place for people to discuss.
- Blog: Each user may share their thoughts as a personal blog.
- Talk: A live chat system that exists in several places, most importantly the lobby.

#### Liveliness

Liveliness of the website can be achieved by adding noisey contents such as:

- As "Today" contents:
  - Daily polls, quizzes, and trivia
  - Daily users birthday
- As "On this day" contents:
  - "... happened"
  - "... was born"
  - "... was released"
  - "... won award at ..."
- To tell you surrounding activities
  - Currently online users
  - Recently registered users
  - "[SOME PLACE] 8 people here"
  - Hot posts
  - Community timeline
    - "user ... just obtained ..."
    - "posts '...' by author ... just received 241 likes"
    - "30 people is active on billiard right now"
- Live chat for a casual real-time discussion; placed in lobby or homepage
- Shoutbox for non-real-time text message, designed for hyping or reacting things out
- Notifications frequently fired to users for any updates around the community
- Dynamic website theme available.

#### Gamification

Gamification essence of the platform adds liveliness and enhance the social aspect of the platform. There are stuffs like level/EXP, coins, badges collections; and system like quests, ranking, and attendance.

For instance:

- Logging in on a specific day gives an exclusive badge
- Coins can be spent to purchase stuffs used in other part of the website

#### K-pop

Bloom K-pop is a fun thematic section that is developed for personal atttachment reasons.

A separate profile option can be made for Bloom K-pop to avoid mixing K-pop terminology with the general profile.

Example of K-pop integration with Bloom:

- Bias entries or favorite song in the profile
- A lightweight wiki-like system:
  - Each artists or groups has a dedicated page.
  - The page contains sections:
    - Main section: introduces the group.
    - Members section: introduces each members and random facts.
    - Timeline section: a visual graph of what happening to the group.
    - Discography section: a discography explorer.
- As widgets:
  - Calendar containing various idols birthday, anniversary, live K-pop events, and other commemoration.
  - Chart and MV views tracker.
  - Live event countdown.
- A watching room for live reaction of an ongoing K-pop events.
- Possibly data of past K-pop events such as music shows lineup and wins.

Data presented can be illustrative only.
